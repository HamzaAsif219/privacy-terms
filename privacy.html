Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobiautopost application ("the Application") handles data. The Application is a personal tool used by its operator to publish content to the operator's own social media accounts.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the Application, it receives an access token and refresh token for the connected account(s) to publish content on the operator's behalf. It may read basic account information (such as the account nickname) to confirm the connection. The Application does not collect personal data from any other users.

2. How Information Is Used

Tokens are used solely to publish content to the authorized account via official platform APIs. They are stored securely (encrypted at rest) and are never shared with third parties or used for advertising.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any accessed data with third parties. Data is transmitted only to the official platform APIs required to publish content.

4. Data Retention & Revocation

Stored tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate the Application and are removed when an account is disconnected. You may revoke the Application's access at any time from the platform's app-permissions settings, which invalidates the stored tokens.

5. Security

Credentials are encrypted at rest. We take reasonable measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access.

6. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.

mobiautopost

Automated social media posting for our own accounts.

mobiautopost is a personal application that automatically publishes news videos to our own TikTok account using the official TikTok Content Posting API. It authorizes the account once via TikTok Login Kit and uploads video content directly to the authorized profile.
